Scientific Dataset Mapping
==========================
Water/climate layers:
- Flood extent mapping:
  Source: Sentinel-1 SAR only.
  Methods: VV/VH thresholding and change/wetness proxies.
  Important: Do not use Sentinel-2 for operational flood detection.

- Sediment plumes / turbidity:
  Source: Sentinel-2 SR Harmonized.
  Methods: NDWI/MNDWI water masking, NDTI/red-band ratios.
  Important: proxy only, not calibrated NTU.

- Algal blooms / chlorophyll-a:
  Source: Sentinel-3 OLCI.
  Methods: NDCI-style band ratio using OLCI red/red-edge radiance bands.
  Important: proxy only unless regionally calibrated.

- Water quality proxy:
  Source: derived from Sentinel-2 turbidity + Sentinel-3 chlorophyll proxies.
  Important: not direct chemistry or drinking-water safety.

- Soil moisture:
  Source: SMAP L4 preferred; Sentinel-1 SAR proxy fallback.
  Important: SAR moisture proxy requires calibration for operational use.

- Drought indices:
  Source: CHIRPS + TerraClimate.
  Methods: SPI/SPEI/PDSI-like proxies.
  Important: keep as climate index/proxy, not crop-yield score.

- Glacial retreat:
  Source: Sentinel-2 + Landsat.
  Methods: NDSI/snow/ice area proxy and temporal retreat.
  Important: only meaningful in glaciated or persistent snow regions.

- Historical water:
  Source: JRC Global Surface Water.
  Methods: occurrence, seasonality, recurrence, transition, yearly/discrete history.

Agriculture layers:
- Cropland extent:
  Source: GOOGLE/DYNAMICWORLD/V1.
  Method: crops probability threshold/frequency/area summary over AOI.
  Chart: yearly line/area chart, km2.

- Cropland conversion:
  Source: Dynamic World temporal comparison.
  Method: compare year N vs N-1; gain/loss/net separately.
  Chart: gain/loss bars.

- Crop phenology:
  Source: Sentinel-2 SR Harmonized.
  Method: SCL cloud-masked monthly NDVI/EVI composites.
  Chart: monthly seasonal line for latest valid year.

- Food security:
  Source: FEWS NET.
  Method: centroid-based categorical IPC phase lookup.
  Chart: categorical risk blocks.
  Important: never interpolate, never convert to continuous score.
